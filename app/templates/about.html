About

Logos is a free, non-profit game. There are no ads, no accounts and no money behind it: it is just a game made for fun.

Every logo belongs to its respective company and is used here only to identify the brand. No ownership is claimed over any of them, and they were collected from the public internet.

Many of the logos come from theSVG, an open database of brand logos in SVG format. Some of them were cropped, resized or colour-adjusted to fit the game.

Country flags come from flag-icons, released under the MIT licence.

If you own one of these logos and would like it changed or removed, just ask.
