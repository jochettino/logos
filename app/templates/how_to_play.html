Logos

How to play

  1. You are shown the logo of a brand or product.
  2. Type its name and press Guess.
  3. Right: you score a point and the next logo appears.
  4. Wrong: the game is over, and the answer is not revealed.
  5. Name every logo to win.

Capital letters and extra spaces don't matter, but spelling must be exact. Some logos accept more than one name.

Every logo appears once per game. Your best score is kept between games.
